Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2026

JapaneseFlow respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information while you use it.

Information We Collect

JapaneseFlow does not require users to create an account or sign in. Most features of the app do not collect any personal information.

If you choose to use the Contact Us feature, the following information is collected solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Message submitted through the contact form

Providing this information is completely optional.

Local Storage

JapaneseFlow stores certain learning information locally on your device using your browser's Local Storage. This may include:

  • Learning progress
  • Completed lessons or characters
  • Review words
  • User preferences
  • Navigation state

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Third-Party Services

JapaneseFlow uses EmailJS only to deliver messages submitted through the Contact Us form.

JapaneseFlow does not use analytics services, advertising services, or cloud storage services to store your learning progress.

Advertisements

The current version of JapaneseFlow does not display advertisements. If advertisements are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

JapaneseFlow is not specifically designed for children under 13 years of age. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with the updated revision date.
